# Product Definition: KawalKopdesMBG

## Overview
KawalKopdesMBG is a community-driven monitoring and transparency platform designed to empower village cooperatives (*Koperasi Desa* / *BUMDes*) participating in Indonesia's *Makan Bergizi Gratis* (MBG) program. The platform provides end-to-end supply tracking for local agricultural and food commodities, ensures fair pricing for local farmers and village producers, enables real-time auditability of cooperative deliveries to service units/central kitchens (*Satuan Pelayanan Pemenuhan Gizi* / SPPG), and offers an accessible public transparency dashboard for tracking meal supplies and budget absorption.

## Target User Roles
1. **Village Cooperatives (Koperasi Desa / BUMDes Managers):** Manage inventory, record procurement from local farmers, issue digital delivery orders, and track fulfillment status.
2. **Local Farmers & Village Producers:** Register commodity supplies, verify cooperative purchase receipts, and monitor fair market pricing benchmarks.
3. **SPPG Central Kitchen Operators:** Receive incoming deliveries, scan digital passes, verify commodity quantity/quality, upload photo evidence, and digitally sign delivery confirmations.
4. **General Public, Parents & Civil Society:** View real-time aggregated dashboards on daily meal supplies, cooperative participation, local farmer economic impact, and report discrepancies or quality concerns.
5. **Oversight Bodies (BGN / Regional Cooperatives Agency / Village Government):** Monitor supply chain compliance, procurement volume distribution, and budget absorption analytics.

## Core Capabilities (MVP Scope)
- **Commodity Fulfillment & Order Management:** Facilitates order scheduling and fulfillment dispatch between Koperasi Desa and SPPG central kitchens.
- **Two-Way QR Delivery Handshake & Proof of Delivery (PoD):** Koperasi generates digital delivery passes with QR codes. Kitchen receiving teams scan and verify items, take timestamped photos of delivered produce, and digitally sign acceptance.
- **Fair Price & Procurement Transparency:** Tracks cooperative purchase prices against regional benchmark food commodity rates to prevent farmer exploitation and maintain pricing transparency.
- **Aggregated Public Dashboard:** Displays daily supply metrics, cooperative involvement counts, volume of local produce absorbed, and nutritional supply milestones without exposing sensitive personal data.
- **Whistleblower & Feedback Loop:** Community channel for reporting delivery bottlenecks, spoiled/substandard ingredients, or pricing irregularities.

## Success Metrics & Non-Functional Goals
- **Transparency & Integrity:** Tamper-evident transaction logs for all commodity dispatches and handoffs.
- **Usability in Rural Settings:** Fast, mobile-responsive interface optimized for low-bandwidth village conditions.
- **Accountability:** 100% of kitchen deliveries validated with photo evidence and digital sign-off.
- **Community Empowerment:** Measurable increase in local farmer commodity absorption through recognized village cooperatives.
